      How to play

      One person holds the phone on their forehead, screen out. Everyone else shouts clues. Tilt the phone down when you get it, tilt it up to pass.

      Sixty seconds a round (or ninety, in settings). Then a recap of what you got, what you passed, and a one-line "what it is" for every card so the clue-givers can settle the arguments.

      No tilt sensor? Switch to Tap: left side passes, right side is got it.

      Act It Out is charades. Friends act, no talking, the phone holder guesses.

      Every card comes from real Burlington data: places, dishes, history, and things people here actually do. A production of the BTown Brief.
